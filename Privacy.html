Privacy Policy for xlist

1. Data Collection and Usage

xlist operates entirely offline and locally on your device. The app does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, sensitive user data, or analytics.

2. Data Retention and Deletion

Because xlist does not collect or transmit any user data to external servers, there is no data retained by the developer, and therefore no account or data deletion request is necessary. All checklist data is stored locally in an app-private SQLite database on your device and is permanently deleted if you uninstall the application.
